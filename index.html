01 — The academy

A classroom small enough
to be noticed in.

Kumar Classes is a coaching centre in Kothrud, behind the bus stand area on Paud Road, a short walk from Krishna Hospital.

Students come here for the ordinary, unglamorous work that actually moves marks: a teacher who knows their name, a doubt cleared the same evening, a test corrected before the next topic starts.

The rooms are quiet by design. Phones stay in bags. Lessons run to the end of the hour. Parents get a straight answer when they ask how a child is doing.

03 — The method

Three things,
done properly.

01

Teach the topic, not the answer

A student who has understood a derivation can attempt a question they have never seen. That is the standard we work to, rather than finishing the textbook early.

02

Test often, correct quickly

Short tests are marked and returned within the week, while the mistake is still fresh enough to be worth fixing.

03

Keep parents in the loop

Attendance, test scores and the occasional difficult conversation. Nobody should find out in February that something went wrong in July.
